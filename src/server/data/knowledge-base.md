# RTI Online — Curated Knowledge Base

## Section 1: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### FAQ 1: Statutory Response Deadline
Under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act 2005, the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO) must provide the requested information or reject the request as expeditiously as possible, and in any case within 30 days from the receipt of the request. If the information sought concerns the life or liberty of a person, the same shall be provided within 48 hours of the receipt of the request.

### FAQ 2: Statutory Application Fees and Exemptions
The prescribed application fee for filing an RTI request with Central Government Public Authorities is ₹10 (Rupees Ten only). Citizens belonging to the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category are fully exempt from paying any application fees or additional photocopying fees, provided they submit a copy of a valid BPL card or ration certificate issued by the competent government authority.

### FAQ 3: Additional Fee Demand under Section 7(3)
If the CPIO determines that additional fees are required to provide the information (such as ₹2 per page for A4 photocopies, actual cost for models/samples, or inspection of records fees), the CPIO will issue an Additional Fee Demand notice specifying the calculations. The applicant has the right to appeal this calculation. The period between the dispatch of the notice and payment of the fee is excluded from the 30-day statutory calculation.

### FAQ 4: First Appeal Time Limits and Procedures
Under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act 2005, any person who does not receive a decision within 30 days or is aggrieved by a decision of the CPIO may prefer an appeal to the First Appellate Authority (FAA) within 30 days from the expiry of such period or from the receipt of such a decision. The First Appeal must be disposed of within 30 days of receipt, extendable up to 45 days for reasons recorded in writing. There is zero (₹0) application fee for filing a First Appeal.

### FAQ 5: Application Transfer under Section 6(3)
Where an application is made to a Public Authority requesting information held by another Public Authority or the subject matter of which is more closely connected with the functions of another Public Authority, the CPIO must transfer the application or such part of it to that other Public Authority immediately and in no case later than 5 days from the date of receipt, and inform the applicant immediately.

### FAQ 6: Statutory Exemptions from Disclosure under Section 8(1)
Section 8(1) outlines information exempt from disclosure:
- Information prejudicially affecting the sovereignty and integrity of India, security, strategic, scientific or economic interests of the State.
- Information expressly forbidden by any court of law or tribunal, or disclosure constituting contempt of court.
- Information causing a breach of privilege of Parliament or State Legislature.
- Commercial confidence, trade secrets, or intellectual property unless larger public interest warrants disclosure.
- Information available to a person in their fiduciary relationship.
- Information received in confidence from a foreign government.
- Information endangering the life or physical safety of any person.
- Information impeding the process of investigation or apprehension/prosecution of offenders.
- Cabinet papers including records of deliberations of Council of Ministers before decisions are taken.
- Personal information having no relationship to public activity or interest, or causing unwarranted invasion of privacy.

## Section 2: User Guidelines & Standard Operating Procedures

### Guideline 1: Formatting RTI Requests
- Be clear, specific, and concise. RTI requests should ideally not exceed 500 words.
- Seek specific records, memos, circulars, files, or notifications rather than asking open-ended questions, hypothetical scenarios, or legal interpretations.
- Avoid asking 'Why' or asking the CPIO to justify administrative actions; ask for copies of the recorded reasons on file instead.

### Guideline 2: Payment and Payment Reconciliation
- Payments can be made electronically via UPI, Net Banking, Credit Cards, or Debit Cards through the Bharatkosh integrated portal.
- In case your bank account is debited but the registration number is not generated immediately due to network delays, please wait 15 minutes and use the "Payment Reconciliation" page using your Transaction Reference Number or Draft ID.

### Guideline 3: Tracking Application Status
- Use your unique 18-character Registration Number (e.g., DOPTR/R/E/26/00991) to check the real-time processing stage.
- If additional fees or documents are required, follow the prompt on the tracking page to upload documents or complete payment.

## Section 3: Statutory Glossary & Key Terms

- **CPIO (Central Public Information Officer)**: The designated nodal officer in every Public Authority responsible for receiving, processing, and responding to RTI applications within 30 days.
- **FAA (First Appellate Authority)**: An officer senior in rank to the CPIO who adjudicates First Appeals filed by citizens aggrieved by CPIO decisions.
- **Public Authority**: Any authority, body, or institution of self-government established or constituted by the Constitution, by law made by Parliament/State Legislature, or by notification issued by the appropriate Government.
- **Registration Number**: A unique alphanumeric identification assigned to each electronic RTI application upon successful fee receipt (e.g., `DOPTR/R/E/26/00991`).
- **Nodal Officer**: The administrative coordinator within a Ministry or Public Authority who routes electronic applications to the respective divisional CPIOs.
